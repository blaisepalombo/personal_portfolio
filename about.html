About Me

I'm a Web Development & Design student at BYU Idaho, and I build mobile apps, websites, and cloud systems that people can actually use.

Background

I'm originally from Tennessee, and I'm finishing my bachelor's degree in Web Development & Design at BYU Idaho. I work across the front end and back end because I want to understand the whole product, not just one piece of it.

I'm also a web developer intern at The Marriage List. I improve a React Native Web app, write tests, and contribute code through a professional review process. On my own, I've built a mobile marketplace, an automated resale analysis system, and full stack applications using Flutter, Firebase, Node.js, and Cloudflare Workers.

I'm proud that I can take an idea, work through the difficult parts, and turn it into something people can use. I'm looking for a role where I can bring that persistence to a team and keep growing as a developer.
